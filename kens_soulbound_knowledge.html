Character system

Races & Abilities

Race is obtained through the first-join gacha. Each race has a distinct identity, passive trait, and in some cases an active ability.

Race gacha: first roll is free. Players can reroll up to 5 total rolls; the last result is automatically finalized at the limit. Race is saved using soulbound:race.

Open the Talent Tree through the Status Book. Talent Points are gained from leveling up, while talent upgrades consume player experience levels.

Gacha rules

  • Each roll costs 1 sb:gacha_key and 300 SoulBound Money.
  • Duplicate weapon drops are allowed.
  • The reward is delivered directly into the player's inventory.
  • At least one empty inventory slot is required.
  • The script attempts to refund the cost if the transaction or item delivery fails.
  • A pity system is planned, but has not been implemented yet.
The key is obtained as a reward for completing a Cursed Contract.
Quest system

Accept a random mob-hunt quest through the Cursed Contract Book. Complete the target to earn a Legendary Weapon Key.

Contract rules

  • Quest is selected randomly from the configured quest list.
  • Progress is detected automatically by the script when the target count is reached.
  • The player must finish the current contract before accepting another.
  • Contracts cannot currently be cancelled or replaced while active.
  • Target quantities vary by quest; the intended design is balanced difficulty.
Only the two quests above are confirmed in this knowledge base. No additional quests are assumed from examples.

Project information

About KEN'S SoulBound

A fantasy RPG addon focused on race-based playstyles, character progression, quests, and legendary equipment.

Official description

Build your path. Awaken your soul.

KEN'S SoulBound is a fantasy RPG addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that introduces a progression system built around races, powerful abilities, legendary weapons, and challenging quests. Discover eight unique races, develop your character through the Talent Tree, collect legendary weapons through the gacha, complete Cursed Contracts, earn SoulBound Money by defeating mobs and bosses, and explore custom armor and PvP combat. Every race and weapon brings a different way to play.
